====================================================

Open the visual board in a browser:

This text file is the feature note for each frame. Use it as the Figma file

00  DESIGN SYSTEM
Feature: the visual language used by every screen.
Frame: 00 Design system
Shows the color swatches, both buttons, and the status badges.
Who uses it: designer and developer. It is not a screen in the app.


01  LOGIN
Feature: sign in for admin, teacher, accountant, student, and parent.
Frame: 01 Login
Left panel is the college brand and three promises: face attendance, fees, and both languages.
Right panel is email, password, and Sign in.
Demo password is shown only on the local design, not as a production requirement.
States: empty fields, wrong password, signed-in redirect to the dashboard or the portal.


02  DASHBOARD
Feature: today's school picture for admin, accountant, and teacher.
Frame: 02 Dashboard
Quick actions: Add student, Register face, Start attendance, Collect fee, Create exam, Send announcement.
The actions a person sees depend on their role.
KPI row: students, teachers, present today, late today, fee pending, upcoming exams.
Two charts: 7-day attendance, and fee collection.
Below the charts: class-wise attendance and upcoming exams.
Student and parent accounts do not see this page. They open the portal.


03  STUDENTS
Feature: the student register.
Frames:
  03 Students          list, search, class, section, status
  04 Student profile   photo is the saved face, tabs for overview, attendance, fees, exams, leave, documents
  05 Add student       name, email, roll, class, section, father, address, photo
A row shows the face photo, Urdu or English name according to the language, student ID, roll, class, face status, and account status.
Register face and Update face sit on the profile.
Inactive students stay on record and cannot sign in.
Teachers see only students in their assigned classes.


04  TEACHERS
Feature: staff records.
Frame: 06 Teachers
Columns: face photo, name, employee code, designation, classes and subjects, face status, active switch.
The detail dialog shows the saved face, qualification, salary, joining date, and assignments.
Only an admin adds or edits a teacher, and only an admin can save a teacher face.


05  CLASSES
Feature: academic structure.
Frame: 07 Classes
Classes, sections, subjects, and the academic session.
These records feed students, the timetable, exams, and class fee assignment.


06  FACE ATTENDANCE
Feature: mark attendance from the live camera.
Frame: 08 Face attendance
Left: camera with an oval guide and a status line.
Right: today's counts and the person just verified, with their saved face and name.
A match marks Present at or before 08:15, and Late after that.
The same person is not marked twice on the same day.
A face that does not match a saved face stays unrecognized. Another person's record is not opened.
Verify mode asks for the person first and accepts only that person's face.
If the network drops, the scan stays on the device until Sync saved scans.


07  FACE REGISTRATION
Feature: save one face to one student or teacher.
Frame: 09 Face registration
Choose Student or Teacher, then the person.
Five steps: straight, left, right, up, down. The head must move. One person only.
Save this face stores the face. Nothing is stored before that button.
The same face on a second account is rejected, and the existing account name is shown.
The preview then appears on that person's profile, list, portal, and header.


08  ATTENDANCE RECORDS
Feature: the month register and day close.
Frame: 10 Attendance
Student attendance and teacher attendance.
Month grid: Present, Late, Absent, Leave.
Close the day fills unmarked people as Absent, or Leave when an approved leave covers the date.
Manual entry exists for a day the camera was not used.
My attendance is the same month view for the signed-in person.
PDF download and print use this same table.


09  FEES
Feature: vouchers, collection, receipts, and defaulters.
Frames:
  11 Fees          student fees table, total, paid, pending
  12 Collect fee   student, voucher, amount, cash / bank / online / other
Other fee frames use the same page with these tabs:
  Structure, Student fees, Collect, Voucher, Payments, Receipts, Pending, Defaulters
Voucher and receipt are print layouts with the college name, student, amount, and amount in words.
Overdue rows use the danger badge.
Grace days, late fee, and the bank account are set in Settings, not on this screen.
Students and parents see only their own fees.


10  EXAMS AND RESULTS
Feature: date sheet, marks, publish, report card.
Frames:
  13 Exams     list with publish state
  14 Results   obtained marks, percent, grade, pass or fail
Tabs on the same page: Exams, Schedule, Marks, Results.
A schedule row is subject, date, start, and end.
Marks are entered per student and subject.
Families see results and the report-card PDF only after Publish.


11  LEAVES
Feature: request and review leave.
Frame: 15 Leaves
Columns: person, dates, days, reason, status.
Status badges: Pending, Approved, Rejected.
An approved leave on a closed day is marked Leave on the attendance grid.
The student's remaining allowance is on the student profile and the portal.


12  ACADEMICS
Feature: timetable, homework, and assignments.
Frames:
  16 Timetable     day, time, subject, teacher for one class
  17 Homework      class, subject, work, due date
  18 Assignments   class, subject, title, due date, file
Students open the same items in the portal and can submit an assignment file.


13  ANNOUNCEMENTS AND NOTIFICATIONS
Feature: notices.
Frames:
  19 Announcements    title, body, audience (everyone, class, section, or parents)
  20 Notifications    bell list, mark read, mark all read, remove
Email, push, SMS, and WhatsApp switches live in Settings. The screen itself is the in-app list.


14  REPORTS
Feature: lists for office use.
Frame: 21 Reports
Report choices: student list, attendance, fee collection, payment history, defaulters, exam schedule, leaves.
Filters: from date and to date.
Actions: Show, Excel, Print PDF.


15  PORTAL AND PROFILE
Feature: what a student or parent sees, and every user's own account.
Frames:
  22 Student portal   saved face, attendance percent, fees, results, timetable, homework, assignments
  23 Profile          face, name, email, edit profile, change password, logout
A parent frame is the same portal plus a child switcher and a My children list.
The header avatar uses the saved face when that account has one.


16  SETTINGS
Feature: college setup, only for an admin.
Frame: 24 Settings
Tabs: General, Appearance, Academic, Attendance, Fees, Notifications, Language, Security, Backup, Audit.
General: college name, short name, phone, email, website, address, logo, favicon.
In Urdu mode those name and address fields display in Urdu and still save the stored English unless the admin edits them.
Attendance: how strict a face match must be, Present until, Late until, leave allowance, working days.
Backup: create, download, restore. Restore replaces the database.
Audit: who did what, and when.
Security: minimum password length and user accounts for all five roles.


17  URDU
Feature: the same screens in Urdu, right to left.
Frame: 25 Urdu
Sidebar on the right. Orange language pill reads اردو. The other pill reads انگریزی.
Names use the standard Urdu spelling. Adnan is عدنان. IDs such as STU-00201 stay in Latin.
Every English frame above has this Urdu twin. Build one Urdu component variant rather than a second set of layouts.
